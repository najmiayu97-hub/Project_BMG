# Home Page (`/`)

File: `src/App.jsx`

## Structure

The home page consists of several sections:

1. **Hero** – Large title "WE CREATE EXPERIENCES THAT MATTER.", BMG description, "Explore Our Work" and "Let's Work Together" buttons.
2. **About** – BMG explanation, statistics (10+ events, 20+ brands, 5+ years, 100K+ audience).
3. **Services** – 4 service cards (Event Planning, Production & Logistics, Media Buying, Digital & Social Media).
4. **Work/Portfolio** – 4 featured projects linking to the Portfolio page.
5. **Clients** – Text logos: JanSport, Coway, Lumi, Serasa Bakehouse, Tel-Popek.
6. **Why BMG** – 5 reasons (Creative Thinking, Strategic Execution, Integrated Solutions, On-Ground Experience, Result-Driven).
7. **Process** – 6 steps: Discover, Strategize, Create, Produce, Execute, Amplify.
8. **Testimonial** – Client quote + 3 photos.
9. **CTA** – "HAVE AN EVENT IN MIND?" with "Start a Project" button.
10. **Footer** – Company info, links, services, contact.

## Data

Data arrays are defined in `App.jsx`: `images`, `services`, `projects`, `reasons`, `process`. Content is localized per language (ID/EN/ZH).

## Header

The home header has inline nav (Home, About Us, Services, Portfolio) and a contact button, different from the `SiteHeader` used on other pages.
