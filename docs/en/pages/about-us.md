# About Us Page (`/about-us`)

File: `src/AboutUs.jsx`

## Structure

1. **Hero** – "ABOUT US" title with BMG description.
2. **Who We Are** – BMG as a strategic partner + activity photo.
3. **Stats** – 4 statistics (10+ events, 20+ brands & partners, 5+ years, 100K+ audience) with icons.
4. **Team** – "People Behind Great Events" + team photo + "Meet the Team" button (mailto).
5. **Values** – 3 cards: Mission, Vision, Values (Creativity, Integrity, Collaboration, Excellence, Result Driven).
6. **Footer** – Footer specific to the About page.

## Icons

The `AboutIcon` component renders SVG for: calendar, handshake, star, people, target, eye, diamond.

## Header/Footer

This page uses its own header/footer (`AboutHeader`, `AboutFooter`), not `SiteChrome`.
